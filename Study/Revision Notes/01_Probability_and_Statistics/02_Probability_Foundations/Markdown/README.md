# Probability Foundations — compact revision packet

Prepared 1 November 2026. All six PNGs follow the compact Counting reference style. Materials ready; study hours and checkpoint attempt unreported.

## Scope and sequence

Probability axioms; sample spaces and events; mutually exclusive and independent events; joint, marginal and conditional probability; total probability and Bayes' theorem. Counting is the prerequisite. Conditional expectation/variance and distributions belong to later packets.

1. [00 — Compact overview](00_Compact_Overview.md): formulas, conditions and common traps.
2. [01 — Worked methods and Bayes](01_Worked_Methods_and_Bayes.md): a two-way table, conditional probabilities, independence, sequential draws, total probability and posterior updates.
3. [02 — Verified DA examples](02_Verified_DA_Examples.md): DA 2024 Q12/Q58 and DA 2025 Q31, plus a clearly labeled other-exam supplement.
4. [03 — Checkpoint Q1–12](03_Checkpoint_Q1_12.md): authored practice with no answers.
5. [04 — Solutions Q1–6](04_Solutions_Q1_6.md): full questions/options, reasoning and answers.
6. [05 — Solutions Q7–12](05_Solutions_Q7_12.md): full questions, reasoning and answers.

## Print and use

Print the sibling PNG folder in filename order, portrait, Fit to page. All six pages are together for one print job. Use separate paper for checkpoint working, then compare the full-question solution sheets. Suggested checkpoint time: 45–55 minutes.

The checkpoint is authored learning practice and includes familiar methods/examples; it is not an official or unseen assessment. Q4 and Q12 are short written responses. No optional challenge is required for this packet.

Read a worked example, cover its solution and reproduce the steps. After the checkpoint, send answers and working, uncertain items, actual focused hours and remaining session availability. The next packet to prepare is Random Variables, Expectation and Discrete Distributions.
