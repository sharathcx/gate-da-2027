# GATE DA 2027 study archive

Personal revision workspace for GATE Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, with a target of 90+ marks.

Start with the [revision guide](<Study/Revision Notes/README.md>). Follow the [general revision SOP](<Study/Revision Notes/SOP.md>) when preparing or updating topic packets.

## Contents

- [Official DA 2027 syllabus](Study/DA_GATE2027_Syllabus.pdf).
- [Resources](Study/Resources/): supplied question bank, DA 2024–2026 papers and final keys, and supplementary resource links.
- [Revision notes](<Study/Revision Notes/>): editable Markdown and compact printable PNG sheets, including separate checkpoints and solutions.
- [Coverage](<Study/Revision Notes/Coverage.md>) and [progress](<Study/Revision Notes/Progress.md>): material readiness and user-reported study progress.
- [Workspace instructions](AGENTS.md).

Superseded study materials remain in the revision archive. Prepared materials do not imply completed study; actual hours, attempts and scores are recorded only from user check-ins.

## Local-only files

The `gate documents/` folder contains personal and administrative documents and is excluded from Git. Local `tmp/` and `work/` production files, credential files and caches are also excluded.

The 1.13 GB other-exam question-paper ZIP remains local and is excluded from this repository at the user's request. Its original location and filename are preserved: `Study/Resources/Previous year question paper of all gate exams/drive-download-20261031T133442Z-1-001.zip`. The other study resources are included with their original filenames.

Source question papers, keys and third-party resources remain subject to their respective owners' rights. This repository is a private study archive.
